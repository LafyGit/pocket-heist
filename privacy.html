Privacy policy

Updated 16 November 2026 · Pocket Heist for iPhone and iPad, by Lafi AlMutlaq

The short version

Pocket Heist has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer does not collect any personal data. From version 1.1 the game shows a few ads, provided by Google AdMob, on its menu screens.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (which contracts you've finished, volume, difficulty) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (version 1.1 and later)

Ads appear only on the title and results screens, never during play. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and information about how ads are shown and tapped, to deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The game asks before ads may use your data for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game also asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change the tracking choice at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Content updates

When you open the game it checks this website for updated game content (for example new text, art or fixes) and downloads it if there is one. This request contains no personal information and no identifier. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Pocket Heist is suitable for all ages. The developer collects no information from anyone, including children; advertising data is handled by Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the version it applies to is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
