Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Pocket Heist for iPhone and iPad, by Lafi AlMutlaq

The short version

Pocket Heist does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (which contracts you've finished, volume, difficulty) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Content updates

When you open the game it checks this website for updated game content (for example new text, art or fixes) and downloads it if there is one. This request contains no personal information and no identifier. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Pocket Heist is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added in a later version, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
