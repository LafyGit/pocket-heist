Getting help

Found a bug, stuck on a contract, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you.

How to play

  • Drag on the left of the screen to walk; push past the ring to hurry. Tap a place to walk there.
  • Tap the action button to use what's in front of you. Tap the footprints to sneak.
  • Tap the map button to pause and plan: it shows what you've learned and the ways in.
  • Game controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, MFi) work too.

No sound?

Pocket Heist respects your phone's silent switch. Turn it off, and check the volumes in the game's Settings.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
