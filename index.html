pocket heist.

Not a thief, exactly. A guest.

Pocket Heist is a small isometric stealth caper for iPhone and iPad. Walk in as a guest, learn how the place works, and slip out with something small and remarkable before anyone notices it's gone.

Get Pocket Heist on the App Store

Three contracts

  • 01 · The Indigo Bird. A private gallery at an evening viewing. Lift a blue ceramic bird from an alarmed plinth.
  • 02 · The Velvet Fox. An old theatre mid-rehearsal. Take a clockwork fox while the orchestra covers your steps.
  • 03 · The Midnight Pearl. A night train at a rainy platform. Pocket a pearl brooch from a guarded private saloon.

How it plays

  • Watch the staff, read what's lying around and chat to people. What you learn opens new ways in.
  • Pause any time to see the plan: what you know and the routes you've found.
  • Touch controls, or an Xbox, PlayStation or MFi controller.
  • No account, and no internet needed to play. Progress stays on your device.

What it costs

Contract 01 is free to play. One in-app purchase opens Contracts 02 and 03, and any that follow, and removes the ads. Ads only appear on the title and results screens, never during play.
