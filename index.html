pocket heist.

Not a thief, exactly. A guest.

Pocket Heist is a small isometric stealth caper for iPhone and iPad. Walk in as a guest, learn how the place works, and slip out with something small and remarkable before anyone notices it's gone.

Three contracts: a private gallery, a theatre mid-rehearsal and a night train at a rain-lashed platform. No accounts, no internet needed to play.
